    System design

    From pixels to a pass.

    The planner schedules observations. The critic checks their freshness, visibility, and maneuver feasibility before granting a pass proposal.

    1. ◉

      Sense

      Project instance masks and depth into lane coordinates. Track gaps across frames.

    2. ⋯

      Plan

      Choose tools to refresh a mutable belief. Compare rule-based and language-model planners.

    3. ✓

      Verify

      Check sight distance, rear headway, the blind spot, and the available corridor.

    4. ↝

      Execute

      Track waypoints, monitor changing gaps, and either merge back or abort.
